# Zemanim Hamitzriyim

These are shitot that I and only I hold by. This is not meant for public use.

### Differences between this and Zemaneh Yosef

- Amidah - De'ah Ubina WeHaskel
- Amidah - Lameshubadim (credits: AS)
- Morning Berachot - Over La'asiyatan (order taken from Siddur HaRambam)
- Morning Berachot - No Hanoten La'ayef Kowakh
- Morning Berachot - No rooster (because I don't live in an area with one)
- Arvit - LeDavid is said prior to the Hashem Tzevaot
- Arvit - Hashem Tzevaot is surrounded by the London S&P shir hama'alot, used also by Egyptians
- No Tachanun - Yehi Shem removes the Pasuk of "Ram Al Kol"
- Anything to do with Rabbenu Tam is completely removed, with the exception of Zemaneh Pesah. For reference, this is Sof Zeman Shema MG"A & Rabbenu Tam itself
- Plag Y"Y is removed
- Seasonal minutes are swapped with full degrees
- Tzet Shabbat is measured by 27 minutes in the summer months of Bagdad
